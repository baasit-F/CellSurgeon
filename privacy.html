What we collect

When you ask for a quote

Our quote form asks for your device or model, what's wrong with it, your name, your phone number and your email. We also note which page of our site you sent it from.

When you schedule a repair

The Schedule Repair page asks for the day and time you'd like, the service you need, your device, your name and your phone number. Your email and any notes are optional.

When you book through our chat helper

If you book an appointment in the chat, we receive the day and time you picked, the service, your device, your answers to the chat's questions about the problem (for example, what the device is doing or which parts you'd prefer), your name and your phone number.

Everyday questions you type into the chat are answered right in your browser and aren't sent to us. Only a booking you choose to send reaches us.

How you use our website

Like most websites, we use cookies and similar technologies to collect information about visits, such as the pages you view, the type of device and browser you use, your general location based on your IP address, and whether you came from one of our ads. See "Cookies and advertising" below.

Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts and show a Google Map of our location. When those load, your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address, and the map may set Google's own cookies.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your quote request with a price
  • To confirm, change or follow up on your appointment
  • To run the shop and improve our website and services
  • To advertise our services, including measuring how well our ads work

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We only share it with the companies that help us run the site:

  • Netlify hosts this website and handles our forms, so the details you send through a form are stored with Netlify and passed on to us.
  • Google provides Google Analytics, which helps us understand how people use the site, and Google Ads, which helps us show and measure our ads. It also provides the fonts and map mentioned above.
  • Meta provides the Meta Pixel, which helps us measure and show our ads on Facebook and Instagram.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Cookies and advertising

Google and Meta may use cookies and similar technologies on our site to measure how our ads perform and to show you ads for Tech Care, and others, on their platforms and across the web. They handle that information under their own privacy policies.

You can control the ads you see:

  • Google: adssettings.google.com
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram): Meta's ad preferences

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work, though some ads and measurements won't.

Do Not Track

Some browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal. Our website doesn't currently respond to those signals.

Your choices

Want to see, correct or delete the details you've sent us? Email info@techcarecollectibles.com or call (302) 609-4060 and we'll help.

Children

Our website isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has sent us their details, contact us and we'll delete them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

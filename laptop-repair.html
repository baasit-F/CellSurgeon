Questions

Good to know

Still curious? Our chat helper can answer more, or give us a call.

How much does laptop repair cost?
It depends on the model and the part, so we give you a free quote after a quick look. For many repairs you can choose between part options at different prices, and we price match official quotes from other shops.
Will my files be safe?
Most hardware repairs, like a screen, battery or keyboard, don't involve your files. If you can, back up first. If your laptop won't turn on, tell us and we'll talk through your options.
Do you fix MacBooks?
Yes, MacBook Air and MacBook Pro, along with Windows laptops and Chromebooks.
How long does a laptop repair take?
We'll give you a time estimate with your quote. If a part has to be ordered, we'll let you know upfront.
Do repairs come with a warranty?
Every part we install comes with our standard lifetime warranty. It covers defects in the part or in our workmanship: if a part fails because of a defect, we'll repair or replace it. It doesn't cover accidental damage (like drops, cracks or water) or normal wear and tear.

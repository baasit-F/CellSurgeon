Questions

Good to know

Still curious? Our chat helper can answer more, or give us a call.

What's included in the $70 screen deal?
Screen repairs are $70 or less on every model except the iPhone 18 lineup and the very newest releases, because screens for those aren't available yet. Send your model for an exact price.
How long does a phone repair take?
Many common fixes, like screens and charging ports, are done while you wait, often in under an hour. We'll give you a time estimate for your repair with your quote.
Will I lose my photos and data?
Screen, battery and charging port repairs don't touch your data. We still recommend a backup (iCloud or Google) before any repair if you can.
Do repairs come with a warranty?
Every part we install comes with our standard lifetime warranty. It covers defects in the part or in our workmanship: if a part fails because of a defect, we'll repair or replace it. It doesn't cover accidental damage (like drops, cracks or water) or normal wear and tear.
Do you price match?
Yes. Bring in an official quote from another repair provider and we'll match their price.
